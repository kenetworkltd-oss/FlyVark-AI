FlyVark AI is a specialized automation agency that transforms manual business bottlenecks into scalable, 24/7 autonomous systems. We specialize in building custom AI voice agents—like Sarah—tailored for the Medical, Legal, Property Management, and Home Service sectors.

Our Star Agent: Sarah
Sarah is a high-performance AI receptionist capable of handling end-to-end customer lead generation and appointment booking. During live stress testing, Sarah successfully managed complex US-based service calls with high transcription accuracy and direct calendar integration.

Technical Architecture Workflow:

Voice Engine: Retell AI -- Automation: Make.com -- Booking: Cal.com -- Notification: Gmail API

Key Capabilities
Intelligent Data Extraction: Automatically captures Customer Name, Address, Phone Number, and Issue Description directly from conversation transcripts.

US City Knowledge Base: Integrated with a comprehensive database of US cities and states to ensure 100% address verification accuracy.

Smart Scheduling: Real-time integration with business calendars, respecting operating hours (8:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST) and preventing double bookings.

Automated Lead Routing: Instant email notifications to business owners (e.g., ken@flyvark.com) containing structured lead summaries.
